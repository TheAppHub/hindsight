## A Brand Built for Finer Details

Finer Timber Doors & Joinery came to us with a proud family legacy and a long-standing reputation among cabinetmakers and designers. They needed a brand refresh that would unify their print, digital and marketing presence, while staying true to their roots in craftsmanship and service.


### A Finer Identity, From Logo to Launch

We delivered a full creative rollout, including a refined logo, natural-toned colour palette, modern website, brochures and social templates. Every touchpoint was designed to balance precision with warmth — a polished, professional identity that still feels unmistakably handcrafted.
