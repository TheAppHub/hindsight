## Rebranding Built to Last.

AHS Group came to us with a clear vision - refresh their brand identity without losing the recognition and credibility they’d earned across the construction and machinery industry. The challenge? Create a confident, future-ready brand that worked just as hard as they do, across everything from social feeds to site signage.

### Built for the Long Haul.


We reimagined their visual identity from the ground up, delivering a bold new logo, flexible brand elements and clear guidelines to ensure consistency in every application. From vehicle livery to billboards, every asset was designed to scale, with considered design thinking behind every colour, shape and application.

The result is a refined and recognisable brand presence that stays true to their heritage while stepping firmly into the future.
